Runs

Error columns compare each run's word start times with your tapped reference (below): error is the median distance, bias is the average offset (positive means the run is late, so try a negative offset), ±100 is the share of words within 100 ms, timed is the share of reference words the run timed at all.

Reference timing

Tap along to make a reference: while tapping, press Space or J at the start of each highlighted word, Backspace to undo, P to pause. Click a word to tap from there. A slower speed helps. Tap delay is subtracted from every tap if you tend to tap late. A few lines is enough to score runs.
